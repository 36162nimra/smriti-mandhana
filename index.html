From Sangli to the World

The Roots

Born into a family where cricket was a shared language, Smriti Mandhana's story began in Mumbai and blossomed in Sangli. Inspired by her brother’s left-handed stance, she mirrored him—unknowingly creating a style that would one day be compared to the greatest southpaws in the history of the game.

By the age of nine, the talent was undeniable. Her family became her first team—her father managing her path and her mother meticulously crafting her nutrition. She was a prodigy who didn't just play; she belonged.

Beyond the numbers, Smriti Mandhana has been the ICC Women's Cricketer of the Year twice (2018 and 2021), a feat that places her among the absolute immortals of the sport. She led Royal Challengers Bangalore to their maiden title, cementing her place as a leader of destiny.
