Smriti Mandhana – Early Life

Elegance in Motion

Smriti Mandhana was born on July 18, 1996, in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. From a very young age, she showed interest in cricket and often played with boys in her neighborhood. Her parents, Shrinivas and Smita Mandhana, supported her passion for cricket and encouraged her to train professionally. She attended Shri Nirmala Convent School and later joined the Mumbai Cricket Association (MCA) Academy, where she honed her skills. Her dedication and talent soon made her a standout player in domestic cricket, representing Maharashtra in various tournaments. Smriti’s early exposure to competitive cricket helped her develop the technique, confidence, and discipline that would later take her to the international stage, making her one of the most celebrated women cricketers in India.

Smriti Mandhana - Cricket Career

The Roots

Smriti Mandhana started playing competitive cricket at a young age in Maharashtra, representing her state in domestic tournaments like the Senior Women’s One-Day Trophy and T20 Trophy. Her talent was quickly recognized, making her one of the top run-scorers in domestic cricket. She made her T20 International debut for India in March 2013 against South Africa and her One Day International debut in April 2013 against Sri Lanka. Smriti has played in major tournaments like the ICC Women’s World Cup 2017, ICC Women’s T20 World Cup 2018, and 2020, consistently performing at a high level. In 2018, she became the first Indian woman to score a century in Women’s T20Is abroad. She has received several awards, including the ICC Women’s Cricketer of the Year in 2018, along with multiple Player of the Match and Player of the Series awards. Smriti is a left-handed opening batter, known for her aggressive style and strong off-side play. In franchise cricket, she has represented the Trailblazers in the Women’s T20 Challenge and currently plays for Royal Challengers Bangalore in the Women’s Premier League.
